You are invited to try IntentLock during ordinary work for seven days. It can
interrupt browsing with a full-page lock. It may get your intent wrong. Day 1 is
an independent setup/first-session attempt; days 2–6 are optional ordinary use;
day 7 is optional reflection. No daily quota, screen observation, recording, or
proof of work is required. This is not an assessment of your work performance.

Participation and every answer are voluntary. You can decline, request help,
skip days/questions, disable/uninstall, or withdraw without explanation or penalty.
You do not need to continue to please the owner. Use only work you are comfortable
trying; do not enter confidential or sensitive material for the study.

IntentLock requests broad access to HTTP/HTTPS pages, tabs, storage, idle signals,
tab groups and alarms, and replaces new tabs. Local extension storage can include
intent, URLs/events, reflections, history and diagnostics. This is separate from
Chrome history and separate from notes you choose to share. Local history is not
zero. Keep optional AI unconfigured in a fresh profile: no keys, saved provider,
or local model. The pilot adds no telemetry.

You may privately tally counts and voluntarily share selected counts or generic,
sanitized descriptions. Do not share raw intent/reflections, URLs/domains, names,
work content, recordings, screenshots, exported logs or extension history. You
may skip anything you cannot comfortably sanitize. Broad descriptions can still
identify someone; sharing is not guaranteed anonymous.

Uncheck **Enable behavior & time tracking** in Settings to stop tracking, or use
`chrome://extensions` to disable/remove the extension. Existing data is not erased
by the tracking toggle. **Delete all data** → **Confirm delete** requests deletion
of extension data, not Chrome history or submitted study notes. To withdraw shared
notes, contact the confirmed owner with your random code. Keep the code privately;
without it the owner may not be able to locate your notes. Study notes will be
deleted by the confirmed date above. No participant material will be published
under this pilot authorization.
